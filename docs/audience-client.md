## What this implies for the content

- **Tailor the recognition beat to their lifecycle.** Map the eight commands to the client's actual process and tooling where known.
- **Branding:** stronger Ingage presence is appropriate for a client engagement than for a community meetup. Decide per relationship.
- **Close / next step:** an engagement-relevant ask (a pilot, a follow-up working session), not a community Slack or meetup.
- **Demo target:** prefer a demo that resonates with their stack; the meta demo on this repo still works as a safe, self-contained fallback.
